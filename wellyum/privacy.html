Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 18, 2026

Wellyum ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by Raidea, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Our Commitment: We believe in transparency and giving you control over your personal data. We collect only what we need to provide our services and never sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

Data Type Purpose
Account Information Name, email address for account creation and authentication
Authentication Data Login credentials (passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text)
Recipe Data Recipes you import, create, or save within the App
Health Goal Preferences Your selected dietary goals (e.g., lower carb, heart healthy)
Photos Recipe images you choose to upload

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

Data Type Purpose
Device Information Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers for app functionality
Usage Data Features used, interactions, conversion counts for service improvement
Log Data Error reports, crash logs for debugging and stability
Analytics Data Aggregated usage patterns to improve our services
Advertising Identifier (IDFA) Collected with your consent for ad measurement and campaign attribution

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

If you choose to sign in using Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In:

  • Apple Sign-In: We receive your name (if provided), email address (or Apple's private relay email), and a unique identifier
  • Google Sign-In: We receive your name, email address, and profile picture (if available)

We do not receive or store your passwords from these services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide Services: Process recipes, generate ingredient swaps, and store your recipe library
  • Personalize Experience: Remember your health goal preferences and customize suggestions
  • Manage Your Account: Authenticate your identity and manage your subscription
  • Improve Our App: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and fix issues
  • Communicate: Send service-related notifications and respond to inquiries
  • Ensure Security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Measure Advertising: Track ad campaign performance and app install attribution
  • Comply with Law: Meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests

3. AI Processing and Data Usage

AI Transparency: Your recipe data is processed by artificial intelligence systems to generate ingredient substitutions. Here's what you should know:
  • Recipe text and ingredients are sent to AI services for processing
  • AI processing occurs on secure third-party servers (currently Anthropic/Claude)
  • We do not use your specific recipes to train AI models
  • AI providers may retain data temporarily for service operation and abuse prevention
  • Nutritional estimates are AI-generated and may not be precise

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government request, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to:

4.4 Business Transfers

If Wellyum is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you of any such change.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Where We Store Data

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Google Cloud/Firebase, primarily located in the United States. By using Wellyum, you consent to the transfer of your data to the United States.

5.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls limiting employee access to personal data
  • Secure password hashing (passwords are never stored in plain text)

5.3 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. After account deletion:

  • Personal data is deleted within 30 days
  • Backup copies may persist for up to 90 days
  • Anonymized analytics data may be retained indefinitely
  • Data required for legal compliance may be retained longer

7. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to Know: What personal information we collect, use, and share
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of the sale of personal information (note: we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights

8. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • All rights listed in Section 6 above
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority
  • Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests

Legal Basis for Processing: We process your data based on:

  • Contract performance (providing our services)
  • Consent (where you have given explicit consent)
  • Legitimate interests (improving our services, fraud prevention)
  • Legal obligations (compliance with laws)

  • Meta (Facebook) SDK, which collects device identifiers and app events (such as app installs, opens, and in-app conversions) for ad attribution and campaign measurement. For more information, see Meta's Privacy Policy

Continued use of Wellyum after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Do Not Track Signals

We do not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals, as there is no industry standard for mobile apps. However, you can control data collection through your device settings.

13.1 Advertising Tracking

We use Meta's (Facebook) SDK for advertising measurement and app install attribution. On iOS, you will be prompted via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before any advertising identifier (IDFA) is collected. You can change your tracking preference at any time through your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

If you grant tracking permission, device identifiers and app events (such as installs, opens, and conversions) may be shared with Meta for ad measurement purposes. If you deny or revoke permission, no advertising identifiers will be shared. For more information about Meta's data practices and to manage your ad preferences, visit Meta's Ad Preferences.
